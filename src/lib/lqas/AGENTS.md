- LQAS rounds/lots (lqas_rounds, lqas_lots) build lots one level below the evaluation unit from microplan_entries; risk flags use only recorded evidence (past FAIL results, security clearance, accessibility) — why: no invented predictions.
- Round phone forms carry round_id + lot choice (L<uuid hex>); server validation enforces project/round/lot consistency and exact samples; deviations or incomplete samples remain In Progress; deletion recomputes the lot — why: invalid or removed evidence must never trigger a decision.
- Saved rounds freeze indicator, eligibility, campaign and PPS slots; both classification risks are validated server-side; missing recorded population blocks PPS rather than inventing weights — why: paper/phone exports remain identical and designs are auditable.
- Reports separate saved-round classification from unlinked submissions, label weighted summaries descriptive, and omit unsupported population confidence intervals; seven-day mop-up dates are operational review defaults — why: LQAS classification is not certified population coverage or a universal WHO protocol.
