# CDD case search and MMDP case confirmation

A new tab inside the Longitudinal Beneficiary Records (Cases page) that follows an MMDP case from the moment a trained community-directed distributor (CDD) finds it in the community, through clinician confirmation at a facility, to a fully registered beneficiary with a Case ID.

## The flow

```text
Facility health worker registers CDD (trained for MMDP case search)
        |
CDD case search -> potential case recorded (person details + limb photos)
        |
Clinician at the facility confirms using the lesion staging tool
        |
   confirmed -----> keep at this facility -> beneficiary registered, Case ID issued
        |
        \--------> refer to another facility -> on acceptance there,
                   beneficiary registered under that facility, Case ID issued
                   with the receiving facility's code
        |
   not a case ----> closed with a reason, kept in the register for reporting
```

## What gets built

### 1. CDD register
Health workers with record or manage access at a facility register CDDs under that facility: name, sex, phone, community / ward / LGA / state, CDD code, training date, MMDP case-search training status, supervisor, active/inactive. Listed per facility with each CDD's case-search totals (potential cases found, confirmed, rejected, referred, registered).

### 2. Potential case entry
Recorded against a CDD and the CDD's facility: person name, sex, age, phone, address / community, condition (Lymphoedema or Hydrocoele), affected side, duration, acute-attack history, brief notes, GPS, and one or more pictures of the affected limb(s) or scrotum. Photos go to the existing private beneficiary media store. Works offline through the existing queue and shows a "queued" badge until it syncs.

### 3. Clinician confirmation
A clinician at the facility opens a pending potential case and runs the existing lesion staging tool on the submitted photograph: on-device measurement, ticked clinical signs, suggested stage plus the learned stage where a trained model exists. The clinician confirms the condition and stage, or marks the case as not confirmed with a reason. Decision support only — the clinician's confirmation is what is stored.

### 4. Registration and referral
After confirmation the facility focal person either:
- **Keeps the case** — a beneficiary record is created at that facility, the Case ID is generated by the existing `next_beneficiary_case_id` RPC with that facility's code, and the confirmed staging is copied into the beneficiary's lesion-staging history; or
- **Refers the case** — to any facility in the system, with urgency and clinical summary. The receiving facility sees it in an inbox; on acceptance the beneficiary is created there and the Case ID is issued with the receiving facility's code. Nothing is issued before acceptance.

Every step is written to the beneficiary audit trail once the record exists, and the potential case keeps a permanent link to the CDD who found it.

## Technical notes

- New tables `mmdp_cdds` and `mmdp_potential_cases` (project-scoped, RLS following the existing `is_admin` / project-assignment / `facility_access_level` pattern, grants, `updated_at` triggers). `mmdp_potential_cases` carries the condition, affected side, photo paths, confirmation fields (confirmed_by, confirmed_at, confirmed_stage, stage label, criteria JSON, measurements), status (`pending`, `confirmed`, `not_a_case`, `referred`, `registered`), referral target facility, accepted_at, and `beneficiary_id` once registered.
- One security-definer RPC `register_confirmed_mmdp_case(_case_id, _facility_id)` that creates the beneficiary, calls `next_beneficiary_case_id`, copies the staging into `beneficiary_lesion_assessments`, links the potential case and writes the audit row — so acceptance and Case ID issuing happen atomically.
- New files: `src/lib/programmeModule/cddCaseSearch.ts` (types, hooks, mutations), `CddRegistryPanel.tsx`, `CddCaseSearchPanel.tsx`, `PotentialCaseDialog.tsx`, `CaseConfirmationDialog.tsx`. Confirmation reuses `lesionVision.ts`, `lesionModel.ts` and `PhotoCaptureField`; referral acceptance reuses the facility dashboard pattern.
- New workspace tab "CDD case search" in `ProgrammeModuleWorkspace.tsx`, visible to administrators and to focal persons at their granted facilities only.
